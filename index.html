01 / About

A little about me.

I started programming in 2018 with a small JavaScript Discord bot, following my curiosity and learning as I went. That bot is still one of my oldest public repos, and was one of the reasons I ended up pursuing Computer Science.

Now in my second year at Mapúa University, I turn personal interests and course projects into apps people can use. Though I work mainly with React and TypeScript, I'm currently learning Node.js, Express, Prisma, and database design by building beyond the front end.

I'm still early in my career, so I try to learn by deploying, revisiting old decisions, and improving projects as my understanding grows. Outside the code, I help organize events for my org and connect with other devs. Sweet coffee and dogs are always welcome.
